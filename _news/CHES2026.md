Our paper "Loricae: Upgrading and Optimizing Multi-Party Computation Protocols with Filmy Hardware Enclaves" is accepted by [CHES 2026](https://tches.iacr.org/index.php/TCHES/article/view/12881).

This work explores how filmy hardware enclaves can be used to upgrade and optimize multi-party computation protocols, improving the practicality of secure computation.
